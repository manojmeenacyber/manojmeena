01 / PROFILE

Who is Manoj Meena?

I am Manoj Meena, Founder & CEO of MK Global Nexus, with a professional focus spanning cyber crime analysis, digital fraud investigation, OSINT, AI and cyber security innovation, investigative journalism and healthcare.

My professional interests are centered around understanding the rapidly changing digital environment and the risks that individuals, organizations and society face within it.

Cybercrime and digital fraud are not purely technical problems. They also involve human behavior, awareness, information, trust and responsible technology use.

My approach is therefore multidisciplinary: combine technology with investigation, research with verification, and intelligence with public awareness.
