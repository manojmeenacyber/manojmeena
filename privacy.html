01 / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 2026

Manoj Meena and MK Global Nexus respect the privacy of visitors and aim to handle information responsibly.

1. Information You Provide

If you voluntarily contact us through the website, you may provide information such as your name, email address, subject and message.

2. Use of Information

Information voluntarily submitted through the website may be used to respond to your enquiry, communicate regarding a requested service or collaboration, and maintain appropriate professional communication.

3. Contact Forms

The contact form is intended for professional communication. Please do not submit passwords, OTPs, authentication codes, financial details, private credentials or other highly sensitive information.

4. Website Analytics & Technical Data

Depending on the services configured on the website, basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, referring page or general usage information may potentially be processed by hosting, security or analytics providers.

5. Cookies

This website may use essential technologies required for website functionality. If analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are added in the future, the privacy policy should be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to external services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube or other third-party platforms. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was submitted, communication, legitimate operational requirements or applicable legal obligations.

9. External Links

This website may link to websites operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact page.
