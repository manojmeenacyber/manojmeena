Privacy Information.

Last updated: August 2026

Manoj Meena and MK Global Nexus respect the privacy of website visitors and aim to handle voluntarily provided information responsibly.

1. Information You Provide

If you contact this website through the contact form or email, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, subject and message.

2. Use of Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to professional enquiries, collaboration requests, research communication and other legitimate correspondence.

The website contact form is intended for ordinary professional communication. Please do not submit passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, private keys, recovery codes or other highly sensitive information.

4. Email Communication

When you choose to contact the website through email, your message is handled through the email service used by you and the recipient.

5. Third-Party Services

This website may contain links to external platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn or other websites. Those platforms operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

If analytics or other website measurement services are added in the future, their collection and processing practices will be governed by their applicable policies.

7. Cookies

This website does not intentionally require cookies for its core navigation. Third-party services, if integrated, may use their own technologies.

8. Data Security

Reasonable security practices should be used to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information voluntarily provided through professional communication may be retained only as reasonably necessary for communication, record-keeping or legitimate business purposes.

10. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information when browsing the website. Certain communication features may require basic contact information.

11. External Links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience or professional reference. Manoj Meena and MK Global Nexus are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, services or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
